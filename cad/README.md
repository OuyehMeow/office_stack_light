# CAD

Reference 3D models of the purchased parts, for enclosure design. Enclosure source files go in `cad/enclosure/` when ready.

Sizes below were measured from the STEP files (bounding boxes, mm). Measure the real parts with calipers before finalising fits.

## Seeed XIAO nRF52840 (board)
Files (uploaded from Seeed's official links):
- `seeed-studio-xiao-nrf52840-1.snapshot.5/XIAO-nRF52840 v15.step`: 3D model (GrabCAD, Seeed). Includes PCB, shield, USB-C, antenna, LEDs, buttons.
- `XIAO-nRF52840-DXF/102010448/top.dxf`, `bottom.dxf`: official 2D drawings for SKU 102010448 (non-Sense).

Measured from the STEP:
- PCB: 20.95 x 17.78 mm. Thickness 1.0 mm board, 1.25 mm including bottom pads/silkscreen layer in the model.
- USB-C receptacle: 8.9 mm wide, sticks out 1.5 mm past the PCB edge, top at 4.2 mm above PCB underside.
- Overall: 22.5 x 17.8 x 4.5 mm (length including USB-C overhang, height from PCB underside to top of USB-C).
- RF shield can: 10.6 x 12.6 mm, top 3.0 mm above PCB underside.

## NeoPixel Jewel 7 (LED)
- `vendor/adafruit-neopixel-jewel-7/adafruit-2226-neopixel-jewel-7.step`
- Source: github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_CAD_Parts, "2226 NeoPixel Jewel" (also has a Fusion 360 .f3d). MIT license, (c) 2016 Adafruit Industries, LICENSE in folder.
- Measured: 23.0 mm diameter, LED tops 3.5 mm above the model origin.

## Slide switch SS12D00G3 (power)
- `vendor/slide-switch-ss12d00g3/SS12D00G3.stp`
- Source: copy found in several KiCad libraries on GitHub; this file from hcmos/kicad7_libraries, `3d_data/SS12D00G3.stp` (SolidWorks 2022 export). Original author and license are not stated: treat as a fit reference, not an official manufacturer model.
- Measured: body 8.5 x 3.5 mm, 10.4 mm overall height (pins 3.65 mm below the mounting plane, top of actuator 6.7 mm above).
- SS12D00 variants differ in actuator length (G3 = 3 mm, G4 = 4 mm, G5 = 5 mm). Buy the variant whose actuator reaches through your wall.

## Tactile switch 6 x 6 mm (button)
- `vendor/tact-switch-6mm/SW_PUSH_6mm_H7.3mm.step` and `SW_PUSH_6mm_H9.5mm.step`
- Source: KiCad official 3D library (github.com/KiCad/kicad-packages3D, commit b8b3cfd), `Button_Switch_THT.3dshapes`. CC-BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad design-use exception (LICENSE-KiCad.md in folder).
- Measured: 6.0 x 6.0 mm body (7.2 mm across the leads), 7.3 or 9.5 mm tall above the mounting plane, leads 3.5 mm below.

## LiPo 803040 battery (generated envelope)
- `vendor/generic-lipo/lipo-803040.step`
- No manufacturer model exists for generic pouch cells. Generated by `scripts/make_lipo.py`: cell 8 x 30 x 40 mm, 3 mm PCM/seal zone, two 50 mm leads, JST-PH housing (6 x 6 x 4.5 mm).
- Other sizes: `python cad/scripts/make_lipo.py --t 6 --w 34 --l 50` (needs `pip install cadquery`).
- Vendors vary +/- 1 mm and the PCM zone is 2-5 mm. Measure the cell you receive and regenerate.

## Not modelled
- Magnets 10 x 3 mm discs, 330 ohm resistor, 100 uF capacitor, wires: simple cylinders, draw them directly in CAD.
