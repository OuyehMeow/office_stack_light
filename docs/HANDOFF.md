# Handoff: Office Stack Light

Status as of 2026-11-23. Read this first when picking the project up (person or AI session).

## 1. 项目概要 / Project summary

Palm-size, battery-powered BLE status light for the desk, controlled from the computer.
- States: available = green constant, busy = red blinking (1 Hz), meeting = blue breathing (4 s), plus one custom colour slot and off.
- Hardware: Seeed XIAO nRF52840 + Adafruit NeoPixel Jewel 7 + 1S LiPo 1000 mAh, slide power switch, one button, magnets bottom and side, 3D-printed capsule (opaque base, translucent dome).
- Software: Arduino firmware on the XIAO; Python host (CLI, background daemon, phone-sized web UI, mock device, Teams presence sync).

Agreed workflow and current phase:
1. Software + electronics selection (Claude): done.
2. Enclosure design (owner): in progress. Part models are in `cad/`.
3. Design critique and revisions (Claude reviews owner's CAD): next.
4. Prototypes: parts partly ordered, waiting for delivery.

## 5. 待解决问题 / Open issues found during handoff

These came from comparing the as-ordered list (hardware.md 3a) with the CAD and wiring docs:
1. XIAO ordered "pre-soldered" (headers attached). The wiring and cradle advice assume no headers (wires soldered to pads, board flat in a printed cradle). Either desolder / clip the header pins, or redesign the cradle for about 8.5 mm extra height.
2. Slide switch ordered is Adafruit 805, but the CAD model is SS12D00G3. Measure the 805 (body, pin pitch, actuator length) and replace or adjust the model before final CAD.
3. Tact switch ordered is Adafruit 1490 (6 mm). Check its height against the 7.3 / 9.5 mm KiCad models.
4. Capacitor ordered is 100 uF 35 V radial; larger than a 6.3-10 V part. Allow space behind the Jewel (measure diameter / height).
5. Quantities for two units: 2 XIAO ordered, but only 1 Jewel and 1 slide switch. Add 1 Jewel (2226) and 1-2 slide switches for unit 2.
6. Still to buy: LiPo 1000 mAh with PCM (check JST-PH 2.0 mm), matching pigtail, N52 10 x 3 mm magnets (6 per unit), 26-28 AWG wire, heat-shrink, Kapton, M2 screws + heat-set inserts.
7. Charging while lit: the LED load can keep the charger from terminating (cell held at 4.2 V). Acceptable for prototypes; revisit if the light lives on USB permanently.
